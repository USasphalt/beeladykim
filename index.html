Questions

Before you call

Do you kill the bees?

No. Kim does live removal. The bees and their comb are taken out and relocated to a safe place.

Should I spray them myself?

Please don't. Spraying kills the bees but leaves the comb and honey behind, which can draw more bees and pests. Call Kim first.

How soon can you come?

Call or text and Kim will tell you when she can get there. Customers often say she came the same day.

What does it cost?

Every hive is different. Call or text with where the bees are, and Kim will talk you through it.

Do you do wasps or yellow jackets?

Kim works with honey bees. Call and ask about anything else.
